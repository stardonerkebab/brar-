How the engine scores

Regime ±2 — close above EMA 50 above EMA 200 with EMA 50 rising is an uptrend; the mirror is a downtrend; anything else is range. Momentum ±1 / ±2 — 30-bar price change measured in ATRs; beyond 1.5 ATR scores 1, beyond 3 ATR scores 2. Pressure ±1 — average close location over 20 bars above 60% or below 40%. RSI ∓1 — above 75 or below 25 counts against chasing. Score of 3 or more is BUY, −3 or less is SELL, the rest is WAIT. It is a rules-based reading of the chart, not a forecast.
